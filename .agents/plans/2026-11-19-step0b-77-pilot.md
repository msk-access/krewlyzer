# Step 0b — #77 pilot: WPS donor-support threshold, measured before any final pass

> Task-level plan for Step 0b of `2026-11-18-integrated-plan.md` (§3). Implements superseded
> `2026-11-18-wps-pon-validity.md` Task 7 Steps 1–5 with the corrections in the integrated plan's §4.
> Revision 2, after a fresh-context review (19 Nov): 14 findings, all accepted; see the end.

**Goal.** Before the final per-sample pass is designed, answer three questions about #77's
scorability criterion with real data, while their answers can still change Step 5:

1. **Method and a provisional `S*`.** Does a per-anchor support key — donor depth, donors present,
   or σ against single-sample noise — bring the cross-patient spread of WPS z down to the plateau,
   in both assays? #77 requirement 2 is "σ dominates single-sample noise"; donor depth is one way to
   key it, not the only one, so all three are tabulated.
2. **Granularity.** Is a per-anchor criterion enough, or does it need per-bin support — a new
   per-sample column, which must be added in Step 5 or never?
3. **Affordability.** Does the criterion withhold fewer cells than the `|μ| < 0.1` cut #77 started
   from? If not, the hypothesis behind decision D4 failed and D4 returns to the user.

It also answers whether #77's xs2 asymmetry is batch (§4), by comparing sites **within** an assay.

**What the pilot does not decide.** The shipped `S*` is set in 6c on the final code, with the same
module this plan builds; 6c also re-checks granularity and affordability, because Steps 1–4 change
low-coverage behaviour (below). The pilot's `S*` is provisional.

## Inputs — already on the cluster, no BAM or BED pass

The Step 0 baseline run (`develop` @ `0abb037`, production settings) covers the pilot from one code
version and one data directory: every All Unique donor of both PONs (47 XS1, 21 XS2) and 160
patients, stratified source (IGO / clinical) × assay × half (measure / held-out), 20 per cell.

**How `0abb037` writes WPS, which shapes every definition below.** The writer **omits an anchor
with no coverage** (`total_cov <= 0`): a donor or patient with nothing at an anchor has no row
there, not a row of zeros. And `run-all` scores each table **in place** against the shipped PON,
so every baseline WPS table already carries `wps_nuc_z` and the other derived columns.

## Definitions (fixed before any number is seen)

**Universe and joins.** Per assay, the anchor universe is the PON's genome-wide anchor table
(`wps_baseline`). Every per-sample value is joined on `region_id`; a sample's missing row is
"no coverage", never a join failure. The panel table (`wps_baseline_panel`) is reported, not decided on.

**Support keys** (all three tabulated, one chosen):
- `S_depth(anchor)` = median over the **full** donor list of `local_depth`, a donor with no row
  counting as 0. `S_depth = 0` is its own bin, never `log10(0)`.
- `S_present(anchor)` = number of donors with a row.
- `S_noise(cell)` = donor σ / median patient single-sample noise at that anchor, where single-sample
  noise is the SD of the patient's `wps_nuc` across the anchor's bins after removing the PON mean
  profile. Reported per anchor as the median over its bins.

The same definition of `S_depth` is pinned in the builder in Task 9 by a shared test.

**Cells.** One anchor × one bin. A cell is **scorable** when the provisional PON has a finite σ there.
All denominators are scorable cells unless stated.

**Spread.** Per cell, the SD of `wps_nuc_z` over **exactly 10 patients**, drawn at random (fixed
seed) from those with a non-null z, so the p99 does not vary with n across bins. Cells with fewer
than 10 are **not dropped silently**: counted, with the number of `|z| > 100` values inside them,
per bin.

**Pooled tail.** Per bin, the p99.9 of `|z|` over **all** non-null patient × cell values — drops
nothing, so a bin whose worst cells lack 10 patients still shows its tail.

**Bins.** Half-decades of `S_depth` (and the matching quantile bins for the other keys). A bin with
fewer than **200 anchors** is merged into its neighbour towards the median; the count is in anchors
because adjacent bins are nearly identical (lag-1 autocorrelation 0.986). Anchors and cells are
counted after the 10-patient rule. Fewer than four bins after merging → stop.

**Plateau.** The p99 spread over the cells of the top three bins, **restricted to off-target anchors
(`capture_mask == 0`)**: the highest-support anchors are the captured cancer genes, where patient
biology widens z. The three bins must agree with each other (each within 1.5× of their pooled value);
otherwise stop — there is no plateau to normalise against.

**Inflation.** `inflation(bin) = p99 spread(bin) / plateau`, with a 95% interval from a bootstrap over
**patients** (B = 200).

**`S*` rule.** Per assay, the lower edge of the lowest bin such that it **and every higher bin** has
point-estimate `inflation ≤ 2` and pooled tail no worse than the plateau's ×2, in the **clinical
measure half**. The point estimate, not the interval's upper bound: the guard against chance is the
held-out half, which must pass the same rule independently; an upper-bound rule at 20 patients would
push `S*` up by small-sample width. Both intervals are recorded beside each estimate. `S*` is per
assay — `local_depth` scales differ by assay; the PON stores the assay's own value.

**Held-out confirmation.** The held-out half must meet the same rule at the same `S*` in **every
stratum** (§4). An IGO failure is reported as possible batch, but is still a stop.

**Donor invariant.** The PON's own donors scored against it: observed `RMS(z)` equals
`sqrt(Σ(n_c − 1) / Σ n_c)` over scored cells, with `n_c` the cell's own donor count, to **1e-3**, and
no donor `|z| > 100`. This is an identity, so the tolerance is tight.

**Granularity.** In the clinical measure half of each assay: of patient × cell values with `|z| > 100`,
the share in anchors below `S*`. `≥ 95%` → per-anchor suffices. Reported with the denominator and with
the share of all scorable cells below `S*` beside it — the test is meaningless if nearly everything is
below `S*`.

**Affordability.** Over scorable cells: the share withheld at `S*` beside the share the `|μ| < 0.1` cut
withholds (`μ` the PON's per-cell mean). Also the share of patients' non-null z values each would withhold.

**Batch.** Within each assay, clinical against IGO: p99 spread above `S*` and median `|z|`, each with
its bootstrap interval. Never compared across assays (47 vs 21 donors).

## Stop conditions — each goes to the user, none is a ruling

1. A donor missing or failing `build-pon`'s own completeness check (Task 1).
2. The donor invariant fails.
3. Fewer than four bins, or no plateau.
4. `S*` rule returns none for some assay, or the held-out half fails in any stratum.
5. Granularity below 95%.
6. Affordability: `S*` withholds as many scorable cells as `|μ| < 0.1` or more.

### Task 2 — The measurement module (repository, TDD)

`src/krewlyzer/pon/support.py` — pure functions over arrays, no I/O, so 6c reuses them unchanged and
Step 6's real-data test asserts the same quantity (superseded Task 12). Inputs are dense arrays over the
anchor universe with NaN for "no row"; the module never sees a path.

- `support_depth(local_depth[n_donors, n_anchors]) -> [n_anchors]` — NaN counts as 0, median over all donors.
- `support_present(...)`, `support_noise(...)` — as defined above.
- `bin_edges(support, min_anchors=200, step=0.5) -> list[float]` — half-decades, zero bin, merging.
- `cell_spread(z[n_patients, n_anchors, n_bins], k=10, seed) -> (spread, n_valid)`.
- `pooled_tail(z, bins) -> per-bin p99.9 |z|`.
- `inflation_table(...)`, `bootstrap_inflation(..., b=200, seed)`, `choose_threshold(table) -> Optional`.
- `donor_invariant(z_donors, n_per_cell) -> (observed, required)`.
- `granularity(...)`, `affordability(...)`.

Tests (`tests/unit/test_wps_support.py`), each written to fail first:

- homogeneous synthetic z → every bin's inflation within its interval of 1; `choose_threshold` returns
  the lowest edge;
- a low-support bin with SD 10× → inflation ≈ 10 there, `S*` the next edge up;
- "every higher bin": a low bin passing by chance beneath a failing one does not set `S*` (#77's
  0.30-next-to-1.69 case);
- **the defect itself**: a bin where one patient in each cell carries a huge z and the rest have no row
  — the spread drops those cells, so the pooled tail must catch it and the rule must not pass the bin;
- a donor with no row counts as 0 in `support_depth`; an anchor no donor covers has support exactly 0;
- the bootstrap resamples patients: one patient carrying the whole tail widens the interval; cell-level
  resampling would not;
- the donor invariant equals `sqrt(Σ(n_c−1)/Σn_c)` exactly on a synthetic PON scored on its own donors;
- bins under 200 anchors are merged; four or fewer bins is reported, not computed;
- two different inputs give two different tables (invariant #1).

## Scope notes

- Superseded Task 9 withholds every `*_z` of a withheld anchor; this pilot measures `wps_nuc_z` only,
  and `run-all` at `0abb037` writes no `wps_tf_z`. The criterion's effect on the derived z
  (amplitude, shape) is measured in 6c.
- Granularity passes trivially when `S*` is high; it is always read beside the share of cells below `S*`.

## Review Focus

1. Cells whose only non-null z is one patient with trace coverage — the defect's own shape. Expected:
   visible in the pooled tail and the dropped-cell counts, never averaged away.
2. `S_depth = 0` anchors, likely a large share (an earlier record measured a median of 8 donors present
   of 47, before #79). Expected: their own bin; affordability reported with them in.
3. Plateau anchors that are on-target. Expected: excluded by `capture_mask`.
4. A re-scored table with duplicate columns. Expected: impossible after projection, asserted per file.
5. Mismatched anchor universes between donors and patients. Expected: joined on `region_id`; a patient
   anchor outside the PON universe is counted, never silently scored.
